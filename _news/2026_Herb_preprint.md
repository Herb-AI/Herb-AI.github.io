We published our extended paper ["Herb. jl: A Unifying Program Synthesis Library"](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.09726) on ArXiv! We point out common problems developing synthesizers, and show how to use Herb for your applications and how to customize existing synthesizers with a few lines of code.
